The work

01

Operational management

Assuming full day-to-day command under tailored Management Service Covenants. You keep the assets and secure a stable yield, bypassing the overhead that stalls a good site.

What we take

Floor management, rota, supplier terms, cash control, licensing, health and safety, and a weekly operating pack. Standard turnaround, implemented UK-wide.

What you keep

Ownership of the asset and the equity. The covenant is an operating mandate, not a transfer. Typical term 12–36 months, reviewed on the numbers.

Where it sits

Convenience, food service, hospitality and other high-street trades where the bottleneck is management, not demand. Glasgow first. The rest of the UK on the same model.

02

Strategic acquisitions

Direct purchases of trading businesses — sound, tired, or undervalued. Flexible deferred or baseline buyouts, with a clean split of liabilities.

The preference

Freehold with the trade. The holding company owns the property. The trading business services the loan. Family can live on site and run the shop. Due diligence cost is accepted. It is not a substitute for serviceability.

The structure

Asset sale or share sale, written to the file. Seller clears creditors where the deal is an asset transfer. Deferred consideration where the numbers support it.

The scope

Food and hospitality, healthcare and care, property and lettings, logistics and quick commerce, professional services, manufacturing, transportation, trading and shipping.

03
